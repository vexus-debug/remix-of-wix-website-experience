# Remix of Wix Website Experience

Recreate a pixel-accurate version of the Wix marketing website (wix.com), strictly adhering to the complete full-page scrolling reference screenshots attached (Homepage, AI Website Builder, and Templates gallery).

The full page scroll height is 18,244px spanning 17 complete sections:

1. Global Sticky Header:
   - Wix logo, dropdown mega-menus (Product, Solutions, Resources, Enterprise, Pricing), search toggle, and high-contrast 'Get Started' button.

2. Hero Section:
   - Primary heading in Wix Madefor Display (82.5px, -1.65px letter-spacing): 'Create your future on the leading website builder'.
   - Subhead introducing Wix Harmony with dual CTA buttons ('Get Started', 'Start for free. No credit card required').
   - Live metrics badges: 300M+ sites built, 90K+ sites created daily.
   - Surrounding floating 3D badge graphics and preview cards.

3. 'Wix Harmony' AI Website Builder Showcase:
   - Section heading: 'Website creation, as natural as thinking'.
   - Interactive prompt input bar with simulated live mockup generation transition.

4. Solutions Matrix (Interactive Tabs):
   - 8 selectable solution tabs: eCommerce, Scheduling, Lead Management, Portfolio, Blog, Online Courses, Events, and Payments.
   - Clicking each tab updates the interactive feature showcase, screenshot previews, and CTA link.

5. Website Templates Showcase:
   - Filterable template cards by category (Business, Store, Creative, Community) with live preview modals.

6. Radical Customization Grid (4 Deep Feature Modules):
   - Vibe-style creation (prompt-to-design).
   - Fluid drag-and-drop canvas freedom with pixel precision.
   - Component & animation library (1,000s of elements).
   - Brand styling system (tokens, fonts, color palettes).

7. 'Meet Aria' Personal AI Agent Spotlight:
   - Animated typewriter/ticker switching between roles: 'personal AI agent', 'expert web designer', 'business strategist', 'hands-on collaborator', 'intuitive guide'.
   - Floating interactive prompt chips ('Improve the site’s SEO', 'Create a $250 pay link', 'Make my site look more modern', etc.).

8. Platform Infrastructure & Foundations:
   - Multi-cloud reliability (99.99% uptime), Enterprise security, Technical SEO core, and Built-in functionality cards.

9. Interactive Domain Search Bar:
   - Live search input field with instant domain availability check, TLD extensions (.com, .org, .net, .store), and free 1-year promotional badges.

10. Analytics & Business Intelligence:
    - Interactive metric toggles for Subscriptions, Marketing performance, Real-time traffic, and Benchmark reporting.

11. Day-to-Day Operations & CRM Suite:
    - Interactive cards for Custom automations, Contact management database, and Unified customer inbox.

12. Integrated Growth & Marketing Suite:
    - Tabs for GEO & AI visibility (tracking brand mentions on LLMs), Social media manager, Google/Meta ads manager, Email marketing, and SEO assistant.

13. Mobile Management (Owner App & Dashboard):
    - Device mockups showcasing the on-the-go Wix Owner App alongside the desktop business dashboard.

14. 6-Step Visual Creation Roadmap:
    - Numbered timeline from defining goals, picking a domain, AI customization, to SEO and publishing.

15. Creator Showcase Carousel:
    - Gallery of real featured user websites with hover elevation and external visit links.

16. Expandable FAQ Accordion:
    - Detailed collapsible answers to platform questions.

17. Multi-Column Mega Footer:
    - Complete directory links grouped by Product, Solutions, Community, Support, and Company, plus language/region selector.

Design Specifications:
- Typography: Wix Madefor Display (geometric display sans-serif) for headlines with tight tracking (-0.02em) and Wix Madefor Text / clean sans-serif for body.
- Colors: Pure white (#FFFFFF) background with dark neutral text (#111827), subtle card borders (rgba(0,0,0,0.08)), and soft iridescent pastel accents.
- Responsive layout across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports with fluid animations and transitions.
